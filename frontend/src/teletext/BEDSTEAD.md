`bedstead.otf` is Bedstead 3.261 by Ben Harris, https://bjh21.me.uk/bedstead/ —
an outline font after the Mullard SAA5050 teletext character generator. The program that
generates it and all newly designed glyphs are dedicated to the public domain (CC0); the
author considers the original SAA5050 bitmaps public domain in the UK under section 55 of the
Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
